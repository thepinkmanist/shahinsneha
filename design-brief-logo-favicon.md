# Logo & Favicon Design Brief — Shahin & Sneha Wedding Gallery

## What this project is

A private, password-protected wedding photo gallery website built for one couple, Shahin & Sneha, to share their wedding photos with family and friends. It is not a commercial product — no other couple will ever use this site. The logo/favicon should feel personal and specific to this couple and their wedding, not like a generic "wedding app" brand mark.

Live site: `shahinsneha.givedivine.workers.dev`

## What I need

1. **A logo** — used as a small wordmark/monogram in the site header area (currently the header just shows the text "Shahin & Sneha" in a serif font — see Typography below). Should work at a modest size (roughly 150–250px wide) and also collapse gracefully into a small square mark.
2. **A favicon** — the small icon shown in a browser tab. Needs to read clearly at tiny sizes (16×16 and 32×32px), so it should be a simplified, iconic version of the logo mark — not the full logo with text.

## The couple & occasion

- **Shahin & Sneha** — getting married. The wedding spans multiple events across May 2026:
  - Pre-Wedding Photoshoot — 1 May 2026
  - Registration / Court Wedding — 7 May 2026
  - Reception (Groom's side) — 9 May 2026
  - Reception (Bride's side) — 17 May 2026
- The site is multi-language (English, Malayalam, Tamil), so the families are likely South Indian / Kerala-connected. This can inform ornamental motifs (e.g. subtle traditional/South Indian wedding flourishes) but isn't a hard requirement — don't force it if it doesn't fit.

## Tone & personality of the site

- **Warm, elegant, understated** — not flashy or corporate. Think boutique wedding stationery, not a tech product.
- **Personal and a little playful in small moments** — e.g. the password-gate copy jokes "State the secret words 🧙" and "Enter the magic word to proceed." So there's room for a touch of warmth/humor, but the overall visual identity should stay tasteful and elegant, not cartoonish.
- Copy throughout uses phrases like "A private gallery for our family and friends" and "Made with love for our families and friends" — intimate, family-oriented framing.

## Existing visual language (match or complement this)

**Typography currently used on the site:**
- Display/heading font: **Fraunces** (serif, elegant, slightly editorial) — used for "Shahin & Sneha" and all section headings
- Body font: **Inter** (clean sans-serif)

**Color palette currently used on the site:**
| Role | Hex | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Background (ivory) | `#fbf3e4` | Warm off-white base |
| Ink (main text) | `#2b2620` | Near-black, warm dark brown |
| Muted text | `#6b6459` | Soft brown-grey |
| Gold | `#b0813a` | Primary accent — used for icons, stars, highlights |
| Gold (deep) | `#8f6a2f` | Darker gold for hover/emphasis states |
| Forest green | `#3e5641` | Secondary accent — used for buttons, CTAs |
| Forest green (deep) | `#2b3d2e` | Darker green for hover/emphasis states |
| Warm gradient | `#e8dcc4 → #d9c9a3` | Used behind event cards |

The overall palette is **ivory, warm gold, and deep forest green** — evocative of botanical/garden wedding decor, brass/gold jewelry accents, and warm natural light. Soft "glassmorphism" cards (translucent white with soft shadows) are used throughout the UI.

## What the logo/favicon should communicate

- Elegance and intimacy — this is a treasured personal archive, not a product.
- A sense of togetherness/union (two names, two families, two people) — could be expressed through a monogram (e.g. interlocking or paired initials "S & S"), a simple botanical/floral motif, or an abstract mark suggesting connection (rings, an ampersand, intertwined lines) — open to creative interpretation here.
- Should feel at home next to the Fraunces serif type and the gold/forest-green palette above.

## Practical constraints

- Favicon must remain legible as a **flat, simple silhouette** at 16×16px — avoid fine detail, thin lines, or small text.
- Should work on both light and dark browser tab themes if possible (a mark with enough contrast, or a version with a subtle background shape).
- Deliverables needed: a logo mark (SVG or high-res PNG, transparent background) and a favicon (ideally as `.ico` or a set of PNGs at 16×16, 32×32, 180×180 for various uses).

## Non-goals

- Don't design a generic "camera" or "gallery app" icon — this should feel like *their* wedding, not a photo-sharing utility.
- Don't introduce new colors outside the palette above unless there's a strong reason — the goal is visual consistency with the existing site.
